🎀 HerHub — Girls Essentials E-Commerce System

✨ HerHub is a console-based e-commerce system designed to bring a wide range of girls’ essentials together in one place.

🛍️ Main Features:
• 💄 Multiple product categories including Makeup, Skincare, Hair Care, Body Care, Perfumes, Accessories, Period Care & Gifts
• 🔎 Product search by ID, name, or category
• 🛒 Shopping cart with adding, removing, and quantity management
• 📦 Real-time stock checking and stock updates after purchase
• 🎁 Discounts, promotional codes, and special offers
• 🚚 Delivery area selection and delivery fee calculation
• 💳 Multiple payment methods
• 👤 Customer information management
• 🆔 Automatic Order ID generation
• 📋 Order summary and order history
• 📊 Low-stock reports and store statistics

🛠️ Skills Used:
C++ | Arrays | Functions | Loops | Conditions | Strings | Input Validation | Data Management | Business Logic | Problem Solving

💡 The project focuses on applying C++ fundamentals to build a realistic e-commerce workflow without using OOP.
